# Classroom map artwork prompts

The source images were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. The three room illustrations were cropped from the classroom selection artwork. The island sheets were split into individual WebP sprites and traced from their alpha channels.

## classroom-selection

Original premium 2D game art for Math Nightmare, a dreamlike school adventure. Hand-painted storybook matte illustration with readable dark silhouettes, tactile wood and broken floating stone, subtle film-grain, layered fog, soft light pools. Evocative, slightly eerie, inviting. Cohesive scale and art direction. No characters, recognizable franchise imagery, UI, overlay lettering, labels, borders, watermark, or logo. Use case: stylized-concept. A wide three-panel illustrated game classroom selector with three EQUAL WIDTH portrait compositions side by side, clean divisions at exactly one third and two thirds, each its own standalone classroom artwork to be cropped separately. Consistent camera from the open doorway and three-quarter elevated viewpoint. Left: warm honey amber candlelight, old desks, abacus, stacks of books, arithmetic on the chalkboard including 7 + 5 and 6 × 4, brass. Middle: deep moss-teal geometry classroom, carved stone desks, large chalk circle and triangle, compass and ruler, cool green moonlight with a small amber practical light. Right: dark indigo observatory classroom with moon phases and coordinate grid in delicate chalk, discrete small x and y glyphs, brass telescope, cool star light and small orange candles. Subtle drifting dream fog in each. All strong focal props and chalkboards occupy each panel's center. These are environment illustrations, no other lettering or panel captions. Strongly differentiated colors with a shared painterly finish.
